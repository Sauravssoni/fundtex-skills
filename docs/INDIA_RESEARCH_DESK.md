# Fundtex — India-First Professional Research Desk Playbook

This is a standard operating procedure for a financial analyst, **not an embedded licensed financial feed or investment adviser**. Every numeric conclusion must cite source, publication time, as-of IST, currency/scale (INR, crore, lakh), share-count period and adjustment status. Do not invent missing values.

## Sources and evidentiary hierarchy

1. Primary exchange and issuer: dated NSE/BSE corporate announcements, results, annual and quarterly financial statements, shareholding-pattern filings, insider disclosures, corporate actions, bond notices, credit ratings, exchange circulars. Cite exact opened filing URL and published/uploaded timestamp.
2. Official macro and regulatory: RBI monetary policy/banking releases, SEBI orders/circulars, Ministry of Corporate Affairs official disclosures where legally accessible, Government of India budget, NSDL/CDSL aggregate ownership only as supported by licensed reliable source.
3. Curated professional data terminal or legally licensed exchange/broker price history; record delay and adjustment policy.
4. Press or news aggregators only as discovery leads; verify substantive claims with a primary source.
5. Research hypotheses and self-produced estimates must be marked separately from facts.

## Company research protocol

A. Instrument mapping — registered corporate name, listed entity vs subsidiary, NSE/BSE symbol, series, ISIN, promoter group, split/bonus history, free float, shares outstanding (basic/diluted) with date.

B. Quality of earnings — revenue quality, operating profit, EBITDA margins, cashflow from operations vs reported profits, working capital days/receivables, related-party transactions, unusual/non-recurring items, auditor notes, contingent liabilities, debt maturities, cash encumbrances, acquisitions and goodwill. Never label a company fraudulent based only on an anomaly.

C. Governance — pledge/encumbrance filings, promoter and institutional holdings with disclosure dates, insider transactions, management credibility, board independence, qualified auditors, regulatory actions and capital allocation. Do not assert ongoing investigations without current sourced evidence.

D. Macro/industry — RBI repo/liquidity, Indian yield curve, USD/INR, crude/import exposure, domestic inflation, Indian rupee cost of capital, government procurement/policy; distinguish observed prices from scenario shocks.

E. Sector-specific lenses:
- Banks/NBFCs: GNPA/NNPA, credit costs, slippages, provisioning, NIM, CRAR/CET1, ALM and liquidity; use P/B/ROE or excess-return frameworks where ordinary industrial DCF misleads.
- IT services: deal TCV versus realized revenue, constant-currency growth, utilization, client geography, USD/INR hedges, attrition, concentration.
- Manufacturing/defence/infra: executable order book vs announcements, cash conversion, capex cycles, working capital, payment delays, quality/regulatory milestones.
- Pharma/healthcare: trial status, DCGI/USFDA status, regulated approvals, research pipeline stage, patent/license exposures; never present pipeline revenue as achieved.
- Commodities/energy: commodity sensitivity, import/export parity, inventory gains/losses, policy/tariff exposure.
- Consumer: volume growth versus price mix, distribution, channel inventory, demand seasonality.
- Digital/platform: unit economics, take rate, gross margin, retention, operating cash burn and dilution.

F. Valuation — Indian tax/regulatory/share-class implications; state revenue, FCF, tax, capex, WACC, terminal growth, shares, net cash/debt and sensitivities. Always include bull/base/bear and invalidate scenarios against disclosed updates. Never present DCF as an observed traded quote.

G. Market/portfolio risk — return sample size, volatility, drawdown, 5% historical tail risk, concentration by instrument/promoter/group and sector, covariance/risk contribution, market regime, turnover/charges/impact and liquidity.

H. Forecast audit — use sourced adjusted-price series with Fundtex Forecast Lab. Measure historical Brier skill vs constant 50% and expanding-frequency baselines, interval coverage/sharpness and abstention. Independently verify future performance; do not change a forecast after seeing its outcome.

## Analyst output packet

Executive verdict (research only), evidence quality grade, price/data as-of timestamp, 5 primary sources with exact dates, financial-statement forensic notes, fundamental model with assumptions, sector/macroeconomic transmission, strongest bull and bear thesis, quantifiable invalidators, liquidity/cost caveats, forecast reliability, **ABSTAIN** or next verification tasks, human reviewer signoff.

## Institution deployment requirements

Market-data rights, confidentiality and DPDPA/privacy obligations, retention/access controls, vendor approvals and SEBI Research Analyst/Investment Adviser applicability reviewed by qualified counsel. No live brokerage integration without separate permissions, risk controls and transaction-level human approval.
