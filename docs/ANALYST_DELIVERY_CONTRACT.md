# Fundtex analyst delivery contract

A top-tier financial research result is evidence-linked, decision-relevant, reproducible and skeptical. It cannot become Bloomberg-equivalent through prompt engineering alone.

## Lead with the result

Opening in six lines: objective, executive research conclusion, materiality, as-of IST, source confidence and the next human validation action. No tool marketing and no pretend price target.

## Evidence ledger categories

- VERIFIED_PRIMARY — actually opened dated NSE/BSE/issuer/RBI/SEBI records.
- VERIFIED_SECONDARY — opened secondary documents, with limitations.
- USER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED — user data not audited independently.
- SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE — fictional example, never actual company evidence.
- ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN — analytical scenario or unresolved data gap.

Include source URL or document, original publication time, retrieval time as known, reporting period, consolidated/standalone basis, currency and units (INR, lakh, crore). Do not mark a searched headline as a verified filing.

## Institutional research packet

1. Issuer identity: venue, series, ISIN if sourced, corporate actions and entity boundary.
2. Financial earnings quality: operating cash conversion, receivables/working capital, exceptional items, balance sheet, debt covenants, management and auditors.
3. For banking and NBFC: GNPA/NNPA, NIM, capital/CET1/CRAR, provisioning and funding/ALM, not industrial EV/EBITDA by default.
4. India macro: RBI, government, INR/FX, rates and relevant sector input costs, with alternative explanations.
5. Valuation: disclosed assumptions, peer comparability, downside/base/upside sensitivity.
6. Risk: relevant liquidity, exposure, volatility, scenario stress, costs, drawdown and a suitable benchmark when valid data exists.
7. Quantitative forecasting: time-causal test, naive baseline, Brier and interval calibration if available; disclose failures and ABSTAIN absent verified edge.
8. Skeptic review: the most damaging counterargument and exact evidence that could falsify the main thesis.
9. Handoff: one prioritized analyst action, open questions and reproducible calculation receipt.

## Six quality gates (0–5 each, never self-certified universal outperformance)

- Source freshness and traceability
- Sector-correct financial arithmetic
- Opposing thesis and falsifiability
- Quantification and baseline integrity
- Decision relevance and clarity
- Privacy, licensing and professional scope

Scores require actual opened evidence and independent verification. A local unit-test pass does not establish live predictive alpha. No trading decisions, current market-data access or SEBI advisory authorization are implied.

When something is missing, deliver the supported partial conclusion with a concise list of missing source inputs. Do not substitute synthetic figures for real company filings.
